# 绘本插画

首页、书架和播放器使用同一套三色木刻风格封面。可点击的设计草图见[绘本交互图](picturebook-concept.html)。产品中的标题、时长、播放按钮与扫光均由网页绘制，保持可访问和可调整。

插画由内置 ImageGen 生成，原图比例为 3:2。网页资源是宽 1280 像素的 WebP。以下为重绘提示。

## 月亮邮差

[封面](../web/assets/fox-print.webp)

Landscape 3:2 full bleed original children's picture-book woodcut print. A small russet fox with a triangular snout in a dark Prussian blue postal jacket, mustard cross-body satchel, carrying one cream envelope, walking left across a burnt sienna hillside towards a tiny warm lit cottage on the distant left horizon. A huge butter-yellow crescent moon, small ochre cut-paper stars, indigo sky and an enormous dark blue pine at right. Blunt carved edges, deep shadows and sparse irregular scratches in ink on coarse ivory paper. No text, frame, logo or mockup.

## 星星花园

[封面](../web/assets/rabbit-print.webp)

A cream rabbit with a long upright ear in a cobalt-blue cotton dress tips a mustard-yellow watering can over six tall white star-shaped flowers. Walled garden of dense tall ochre cut-paper leaves and mint/teal hills. Soft apricot sky, one small off-white star in the twilight. Dominant sage green and warm yellow, navy shadows. Side view, expressive movement. Landscape 3:2 picture-book woodcut on rough ivory paper. Broad flat printing inks and sparse carved hatching, no text.

## 小熊的纸船

[封面](../web/assets/bear-print.webp)

A squat cinnamon-brown bear in a red striped sailor shirt lies belly down facing left on an ochre riverbank, reaching to release one single large folded ivory paper boat on a deep cobalt-blue stream. Sweeping curling white carved lines in blue water and two flattened russet reeds. Pale sky blue, rich indigo, cinnamon and burnt vermilion. Quiet, asymmetrical landscape 3:2 picture-book woodcut with blunt shapes, coarse dry ink and sparse carved hatching. No text.
